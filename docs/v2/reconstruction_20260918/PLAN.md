## New mouse controls: prespecification before comparator outcomes

Question: Are the focal synaptic responses unusually negative compared with other perturbations matched on independent neuronal dependency, baseline expression and mouse coverage? Non-targeting pseudo-groups address calibration only.

Use the tracked Tian2021 CRISPRi plus-antioxidant survival percentile as an external continuous dependency proxy; this is not a binary, mouse-specific essentiality label. Scores vary materially among the four focal genes. Map by unique author BioMart orthology. Exclude NAA20/NAA25 and genes in Reactome pathways whose names include proteasom, plus all eight original leads. Do not exclude other biological families because of their observed mouse responses. Candidate targets must have >=8 eligible primary mice under identical metadata-only rules and four observed guide IDs. Preselect by dependency percentile within 0.10 of each focal value and coverage log2 ratio within 1 (twofold); retain up to the nearest 30 by squared distance in percentile/0.10 and log2 coverage ratio, ties resolved by gene symbol. This feasibility shortlist uses no synaptic expression values.

Read target-gene expression only in non-targeting neurons. Mean baseline expression is averaged first within each mouse, then equally over mice, using the same eligible control population. Require baseline >0 for all tested genes. Retain comparators within twofold baseline expression of the focal gene; choose the nearest 20 by squared standardized distance in dependency percentile/0.10, log2 baseline-expression ratio and log2 eligible-mouse-count ratio. Require at least 10 matched comparator genes, at least 8 overlapping focal mice, and at least 5 comparator perturbations per retained focal mouse. No post-outcome widening if support fails. Save the exact comparator manifest and a separate Git checkpoint before any comparator synaptic score is calculated. Insufficient overlap is NOT_EVALUABLE, not a null or success.

For each focal and comparator, use the identical original within-mouse subtype-matched non-targeting estimator. Within each focal mouse, average eligible comparator-gene effects equally and subtract from the focal effect. A one-sample two-sided t test over those paired mouse differences, 95% CI and BH across all four focal comparisons constitute the primary new test. Missing/not-evaluable comparisons enter the family as p=1 for BH and retain missing effect estimates. Report all effects and all comparator genes. A negative effect with BH<0.05 supports a larger decrease than this matched panel only. It does not establish unique pathway specificity or causality.

Report comparator-gene reference ranks descriptively, avoiding genes/cells as animal replicates. Add litter-averaged paired differences and leave-one-comparator-out mean ranges as sensitivities. Also report a broad essential-like challenge using all metadata-qualified external dependency-bottom-quintile targets in the shortlist, excluding proteasome/NatB, without calling it expression-matched when it is not. Keep any lack of balance explicit. No claim is rescued by choosing whichever sensitivity is favorable.

## Boundaries and preservation

Preserve all original null labels, the estimated-correlation CAMERA failure, preprint/source-selection qualifications and small Naa20 effect size.
