# GSE116280 rotenone replication

The prespecified primary contrast, day-15 LUHMES neurons exposed to 50 nM
rotenone for 24 hours, did not independently replicate proteasome assembly at
the declared nominal p < 0.05 threshold:

- NES = -1.311
- nominal p = 0.0958
- Reactome-wide FDR = 0.668

No fixed-panel gene passed FDR in this contrast.

Declared secondary contrasts show context dependence. Proteasome assembly is
negative at day 8 after 24 hours at both 50 nM (NES -1.559, p = 0.0172) and
100 nM (NES -1.500, p = 0.0210), but is not consistently significant or even
directionally stable across every dose, time, and differentiation day.

This dataset therefore supplies directionally consistent but nonsignificant
evidence for the primary mature-cell contrast and nominal support in earlier
day-8 cultures. It does not establish universal rotenone replication.
