# GSE196190 held-out validation

The primary frozen cilium/Hedgehog/microtubule-trafficking union was Down in the
locked 100 micromolar MPP+ sustained-endpoint contrast, but it was not
statistically confirmed:

- 202 analyzed mechanism genes
- CAMERA one-sided-down p = 0.1221
- all 5 constituent pathways had negative mean moderated t statistics
- frozen outcome: `NOT_CONFIRMED`

The simple-filter and leave-one-pathway-out sensitivities did not change the
conclusion. A sensitivity blocking three replicate series inferred from the
submitter filenames was also nonsignificant (one-sided-down p = 0.0692).
Aggrephagy was not tested because its prespecified gate stayed closed. The
complete interpretation is in
`docs/v2/heldout_mechanism_validation_report.md`.
